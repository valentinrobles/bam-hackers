# Lumi — Pitch Storyline

The narrative order for the numbers in [MARKET.md](MARKET.md). Ordering matters more than which
figures we picked.

**The core move:** the audience walks in assuming IVF's problem is cost and science. Our data says
it's loneliness. Do not give that away early — build to it as a reveal at beat 7.

---

## The arc

| # | Beat | What she's living | The number that lands | Why here |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **She decides** | A choice made with joy | *none* | Never open with data. Let them meet a person first |
| 2 | **It isn't working** | A year of trying, then a word she didn't expect | **1 in 6 adults** (WHO 2023) | They just thought "how sad, how rare." Correct that immediately — half the room knows someone |
| 3 | **She starts IVF** | Not a last resort. Just how families get made now | **~1 in 10 babies born in Spain** | Makes her ordinary, and makes it local. One of ~168,000 cycles in this country this year |
| 4 | **The grind** | Injections, early scans, work excuses | **142 hours. 15.6 workdays. €4,300–6,700.** Monitoring every 1–3 days | The weight starts to land. Cost belongs here as texture — not as thesis |
| 5 | **The gap** ← *the product lives here* | 11:40pm, bloated and frightened, clinic opens in nine hours | **Two to three days alone between appointments** (derived from cadence — say "derived") | No statistic beats the scene. Let it breathe |
| 6 | **The breaking point** | She isn't coping, and thinks that's her failing | **~30% screen positive for clinical anxiety or depression** | She's not weak. She's typical |
| 7 | **THE REVEAL** | She wants to stop | **Psychological burden 14% vs financial 9.2%.** They quit **despite odds as high as 72%**, with treatment **already subsidised in 45% of studies** | The pivot. Everything they assumed was wrong. Cite Gameiro out loud |
| 8 | **Why that's the tragedy** | She quits after cycle two | **42% live birth after 3 cycles, 65–70% after 6** | She walks away right before the odds improve. Land it, then pause |
| 9 | **So what would have kept her?** | — | *none* | Cut straight to the demo. The question is the transition |
| 10 | **The business case** | — | Retention, not deflection. One more cycle ≫ saved nurse minutes | Only *after* they've felt it |
| 11 | **Close — back to her** | "Somewhere tonight at 11:40, someone is awake with this." | *none* | Bookend. End on the person, not the ask |

---

## Rules

**One number per beat.** Stacked statistics stop being information and become noise. Each figure
should answer a question the audience formed ten seconds earlier — which is why order matters more
than selection.

**Cost appears at beat 4, never as the thesis.** Leading with €6,000 pre-loads the audience with
the wrong answer, and the reveal at beat 7 then has nothing to overturn.

**Beats 7 and 8 are the pitch.** If we get cut short, those two survive and everything else goes.
Rehearse them until they're automatic.

**Give her a name.** "María, 34, in Barcelona" outperforms "a woman" by a wide margin. In this room
a Spanish name makes her a neighbour rather than a case study.
